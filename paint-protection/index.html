Paint care & protection

What we do.

Every job is assessed case by case and quoted after the assessment. Services are sold individually.

  • Decontamination

    Removes bonded contamination from the paint before any polish or coating.

  • One-Step Technical Polish

    Machine polishing in a single stage: removes light swirls, haze and oxidation and restores gloss.

  • Ceramic Coating

    Ceramic protection applied on clean, corrected paint.

  • PPF (Paint Protection Film)

    Protective film for the paint, chosen at assessment for the areas and for how you use the vehicle.

  • Full Detail

    Complete interior and exterior detail that finishes the job after polish and protection.

We do not sell basic or express washes. Washing is only preparation for polish, coating or PPF.

Questions

Paint care & protection: common questions.

Do you offer car washes?

No. We do not sell basic or express washes. Washing is only a preparation step before polishing, ceramic coating or PPF.

What is one-step polishing?

One-step technical polish is machine polishing in a single stage. It removes light swirls, haze and oxidation and restores gloss.

Should I choose ceramic coating or PPF?

It depends on how you drive and where you park. We recommend one after we assess the paint and how you use the vehicle, and we explain what each one does and does not do.

How much does paint protection cost in Fremont?

The price is set at assessment, case by case. Send your vehicle's year and model and a few wide and close-up photos, and we reply with the next step.

Do you protect motorcycles?

Our services cover cars and motorcycles. PPF for motorcycle tanks and fairings is offered at assessment.
