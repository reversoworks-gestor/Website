The installation path can depend on vehicle trim, mounting geometry, connectors, lighting controls, and the assembly's specifications. We review the specific combination and confirm what is included before work begins.

01 / FITMENT

Mounting and clearance

We review the vehicle application, mounting points, and available space. A similar-looking unit or broad fitment listing is not enough to confirm a match.

02 / INTERFACE

04 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Clear answers, no assumptions

Before you
request a review.

Can any custom or aftermarket headlight be installed?

No. Fitment depends on the exact vehicle and assembly, including mounting points and electrical or vehicle-system requirements. The specific combination is reviewed before work is confirmed.

Is every installation plug-and-play?

No. Some assemblies may require adapters, wiring changes, coding, aiming, or other adjustments. Requirements and included work are confirmed before the estimate is accepted.

Can you confirm road-use approval from a photo?

No blanket approval is inferred from appearance. Requirements depend on the product, vehicle, installation, and applicable local rules. Share the manufacturer's documentation and verify the requirements that apply to your vehicle and location.

Do I need to supply the headlight assembly?

Parts supply is not assumed. Whether the assembly is customer-supplied or part of a separately confirmed scope is agreed before work begins. If you have the product, send its exact part number and documentation.

What should I include in my first message?

Include the vehicle year, make, model, and trim; the headlight brand and part number; a product link or documentation; photos of the current unit and connectors; and your intended result.
