Questions

Common questions.

What is Reverso Works?

Reverso Works is an automotive studio in Fremont, California. We restore, refine and recreate: paintless dent repair, paint protection and detailing, headlight restoration and custom 3D parts for cars and motorcycles.

Where is Reverso Works and how do I book?

We are at 3573 Eugene St, Unit B, Fremont, California, by appointment. Send your vehicle's year and model and a few photos, and we reply with the next step for an assessment.

How much do your services cost?

Every job is assessed case by case and quoted after the assessment. Services are sold individually, with no packages for now.

Do you wash cars?

No. We do not sell basic or express washes. Washing is only a preparation step before polishing, coating or PPF.

Do you do mechanical work or full repaints?

No. We do not do mechanical work, full paint jobs or audio. We refer mechanical work to a partner shop.
