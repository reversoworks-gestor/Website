Questions, answered

Bring the
right question.

What should I send for a quote?

A few clear photos, the vehicle or component, a description of the issue and the outcome you have in mind are a helpful start.

Can you work from a broken or missing part?

For a 3D project, a broken part, a matching interface, photos and measurements can all be useful reference material for the initial evaluation.

Will PDR work on every dent?

PDR can be a strong option when the paint surface is intact and the panel shape, access and dent location allow a controlled repair. Evaluation comes first.

When should headlights be restored?

Haze, yellowing and reduced clarity can signal UV and oxidation damage. A condition review can determine whether restoration is the appropriate next step.
