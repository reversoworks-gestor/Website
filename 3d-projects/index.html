Questions

3D parts, OEM & custom: common questions.

Can you make a car part that is discontinued?

In many cases, yes. We scan or measure the part, design it in CAD, print a prototype, test the fit, and only then print the final part. If a print cannot handle the job, we tell you at the quote.

What is the fit test?

The fit test is an approval gate. The prototype is tested on the vehicle or by you, and nothing is printed for real until the fit is approved. If it fails, the design goes back to CAD.

Do you make parts for motorcycles?

Yes. A Kawasaki came in after a fall with brackets and fairings that were no longer available in the US market, and importing them would have cost too much. We made the parts in 3D.

How much does a custom 3D part cost?

The price is set at assessment. We split the quote into scan and CAD, printing and finishing.
