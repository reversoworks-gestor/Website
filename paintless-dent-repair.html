01 / SERVICE SCOPE

Assessment before recommendation

Review the damage.
Confirm the right path.

PDR suitability depends on more than dent size. Paint integrity, the shape and location of the damage, access to the panel, and prior repairs can affect the recommended approach. We assess the specific vehicle and confirm scope before work begins.

01 / PAINT CONDITION

Protect the finish

Cracked, chipped, or previously refinished paint may affect suitability or call for a different repair path. Finish condition is reviewed before a recommendation.

02 / DENT PROFILE

Shape and location matter

Depth, sharpness, stretching, and proximity to a panel edge or body line can influence whether a paintless approach is practical.

03 / PANEL ACCESS

Assess the repair area

Panel geometry, available access, and surrounding structure can limit the repair options. Inspection helps determine the appropriate next step.

04 / QUESTIONS

Before you reach out

Common
questions.

Can every dent be repaired with PDR?

No. Suitability depends on the dent, paint condition, panel construction, access, and repair history. Some damage needs a different repair method.

Does paintless dent repair require repainting?

PDR is intended to address eligible dents while preserving the existing finish. If the paint is damaged or the panel condition is unsuitable, another repair path may be recommended.

Can you confirm the repair from photos?

Photos can help with an initial review, but they may not show panel access, paint condition, or the full damage. An in-person assessment may be needed before confirming suitability or an estimate.

What should I include in my first message?

Include the vehicle year, make, and model; the affected panel; clear overview and close-up photos; and any known paint damage or previous repair.
