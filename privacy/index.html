Privacy policy.

Last updated November 16, 2026. This is a plain-language summary and not legal advice.

What we collect

When you ask for an assessment we receive what you send us: your name, contact details, vehicle details and photos. We use it to assess the job, prepare your quote and schedule the work.

How we use it

We use your information only to assess, quote and do your job and to follow up afterwards. We do not sell your information. We publish before and after photos only with your permission.

Assessment form

If you use our assessment form, your name, phone number, chosen service and contact preference are sent to our email inbox through an email-forwarding service. We use them only to contact you about your request.

Website analytics

We may use analytics to count visits and clicks, such as clicks on the phone or email buttons. This tells us which pages are useful and does not identify you by name.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete the photos and details you sent. Write to contact@reversoworks.com or use the contact page.
