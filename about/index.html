About

About Reverso Works.

Reverso Works is an automotive studio in Fremont, California, run by Lincoln Porta. We give cars and motorcycles back the look, the protection and the parts they lost, by appointment and assessed case by case.

Restore. Refine. Recreate.

What we do

  • Paintless dent repair: Dents out with no paint and no filler. Your factory finish stays original.
  • Paint care & protection: Polish, ceramic coating or PPF, chosen for how you drive and where you park.
  • Headlight restoration: Yellowed, cloudy lenses restored to clear, then protected.
  • 3D parts, OEM & custom: Discontinued brackets, trim and fairings, scanned, modeled and test-fitted.

How we work

  • Document. We record the condition before we touch the vehicle.
  • Explain. We connect method, material and use, and separate what is possible from what is guaranteed.
  • Compare honestly. Before and after at the same angle, with the customer's permission.
  • Show the limits. We never promise durability, warranty or results without evidence for your case.

What we do not do

We do not sell basic or express washes. We do not do mechanical work, full paint jobs or audio.
