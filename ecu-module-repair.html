04 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Clear answers, no assumptions

Before you
request a review.

Can every ECU or automotive module be repaired?

No. Repairability depends on the exact module, failure, physical condition, parts availability, and required testing. The module must be assessed before a repair path is confirmed.

Does a diagnostic trouble code prove that the ECU is faulty?

No. A code identifies a system or circuit condition; it does not automatically identify the failed component. Wiring, power, grounds, connectors, sensors, or other vehicle systems may need to be checked.

Are programming, coding, or module pairing included?

Not automatically. These functions are separate scope items and depend on the specific vehicle and module. Confirm availability and responsibility before work is agreed.

Can photos or a scan report confirm the repair?

They can support an initial review, but they do not confirm an internal module failure or establish that a repair is possible. Further testing or inspection may be required.

Should I remove or ship the module now?

Please contact Reverso Works first. The assessment plan may require vehicle-side information or testing, and handling instructions should be confirmed for the exact module.
