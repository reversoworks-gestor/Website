Questions

Headlight restoration: common questions.

Can all cloudy headlights be restored?

No. Only damage on the outside of the lens can be restored. If the damage is inside the lens, or the lens is cracked, exterior restoration does not fix it, and we tell you before we quote.

How do you restore yellowed headlights?

We mask the paint and trim, sand the lens from coarse to fine grit without skipping a step, polish until it is clear, and protect the surface.

Will my headlights turn cloudy again?

We protect the lens surface after restoring it, and we explain the next care at delivery. We do not promise a lifespan without evidence for your case.
