Questions

Paintless dent repair: common questions.

What is paintless dent repair?

Paintless dent repair (PDR), also called paintless dent removal, removes dents with no paint and no filler. The metal is worked back into shape from behind the panel, or pulled with glue, so the factory finish stays original.

How much does paintless dent repair cost in Fremont?

The price is set at assessment, case by case. Send photos of the dent and your vehicle's year and model, and we reply with the next step.

Can every dent be repaired without paint?

No. We check paint, shape and access first. If the paint is broken, the scope changes and it may not be a pure PDR job. If PDR does not fit your dent, we tell you.

Do you repair dents on motorcycles?

Our services cover cars and motorcycles. Send photos and we confirm what fits at the assessment.

Do you work with dealerships and used-car lots?

Yes, trade PDR for used-car lots and dealerships is on our menu and is offered at assessment.
