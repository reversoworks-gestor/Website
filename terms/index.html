Assessments and quotes

Every job is assessed case by case. A quote is given after the assessment and applies to the work and the vehicle condition we described together. If the condition changes during the work, we reassess and talk to you before going further.

Appointments

We work by appointment only. Please tell us as early as you can if you need to reschedule or cancel.

Photos and information you send

Photos and details you send are used to assess your job. See our privacy policy. We publish before and after photos of your vehicle only with your permission.

What we do not do

We do not sell basic washes, and we do not do mechanical work or full paint jobs. Our services are sold individually.

Results and care

Results depend on the vehicle and its condition. We explain what can and cannot be done before we start, and we share the next care steps when we deliver.
