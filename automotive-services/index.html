Services

Automotive services in Fremont, CA.

Reverso Works is an automotive studio in Fremont, California. We restore, refine and recreate, with four services for cars and motorcycles. Every job is assessed case by case and quoted after the assessment. Services are sold individually.

Our four services

  • Paintless dent repair

    Dents out with no paint and no filler. Your factory finish stays original.Restore · PDR

  • Paint care & protection

    Polish, ceramic coating or PPF, chosen for how you drive and where you park.Refine · Paint care

  • Headlight restoration

    Yellowed, cloudy lenses restored to clear, then protected.Restore · Headlights

  • 3D parts, OEM & custom

    Discontinued brackets, trim and fairings, scanned, modeled and test-fitted.Recreate · 3D parts

Also offered at assessment

  • Paint touch-up for small chips
  • Dealer and trade PDR for used-car lots and dealerships
  • Wheel, glass and seat coating
  • Exterior plastic and rubber restoration
  • Ceramic coating maintenance
  • Two-step polish for heavy correction
  • Deep interior sanitizing, leather and odors
  • Curb rash wheel repair
  • Window tint within California law
  • Ambient interior lighting
  • Sound deadening
  • Motorcycle PPF
  • PPF on restored headlights
  • Motorcycle parts

These are part of our menu. We discuss them at the assessment.

What we do not do

We do not sell basic or express washes. We do not do mechanical work, full paint jobs or audio.
